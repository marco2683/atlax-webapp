Cookie Policy

Effective date: 20 April 2026  •  Website: www.atlasdt.com
Recommended use: publish alongside a live cookie banner / preference centre.

This Cookie Policy explains how Paniani Products Pty Ltd trading as AtlasDT uses cookies and similar technologies on www.atlasdt.com and any related AtlasDT websites, portals and dashboards that link to this policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, remember your preferences, recognise your browser or device, understand how users interact with pages, and improve security and performance. AtlasDT may also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, tags, SDKs and session identifiers.

2. Why AtlasDT uses cookies

AtlasDT may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • operate the website and essential platform functions;
  • keep you signed in and maintain session integrity;
  • remember settings and preferences;
  • measure usage, diagnose performance issues and improve the website;
  • support security, fraud prevention and abuse detection;
  • measure the effectiveness of communications and campaigns; and
  • where permitted, personalise content or marketing.

3. Types of cookies we may use

CategoryDescriptionTypical legal basis / consent position
Strictly necessaryRequired for core website or platform functions, such as page routing, login sessions, security tokens, load balancing, consent management and shopping / RFQ workflow continuity.Usually active by default because they are necessary for service delivery.
FunctionalRemember preferences such as language, region, interface settings, recently viewed items, or form progress.May be used on the basis of legitimate interests or consent, depending on the jurisdiction.
Analytics / performanceHelp us understand traffic, user journeys, device/browser patterns, platform performance and feature usage.Consent where required by law; otherwise legitimate interests where permitted.
Security / fraud preventionUsed to detect suspicious activity, protect accounts, monitor abuse and preserve service integrity.Usually necessary for service security and legitimate interests.
Advertising / targetingUsed to measure campaigns or tailor ads on AtlasDT or third-party services.Activated only where permitted and with consent where required.

4. First-party and third-party cookies

Some cookies are set directly by AtlasDT. Others may be set by third-party service providers that support analytics, communications, embedded content, hosting, payment, marketing or support functions. Third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Cookie duration

Cookies may be:

  • Session cookies, which expire when you close your browser; or
  • Persistent cookies, which remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.

Retention periods vary depending on the cookie's purpose, technical design and the service provider involved.

6. Managing your preferences

  1. You can manage non-essential cookies through AtlasDT's cookie banner or preference centre, where available: Cookie Preferences">Cookie Preferences.
  2. You can also change your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality, sign-in persistence, saved preferences, shopping / RFQ flows, or dashboard performance.
  3. On mobile devices, tracking and storage settings may also be controlled through your operating system or application settings.

7. Do Not Track and browser controls

Some browsers or extensions offer “Do Not Track” or similar signals. Because there is no universally accepted standard for how such signals should be interpreted, AtlasDT may not respond uniformly to all such signals unless required by applicable law.

8. Changes to this Cookie Policy

AtlasDT may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal or technical changes. The latest version published on this page will apply from its stated effective date.

9. More information

For more information about how AtlasDT handles personal information, see the Privacy Policy. Privacy-related questions may be directed to info@atlasdt.com.
