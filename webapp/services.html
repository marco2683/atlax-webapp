Based right here in Melbourne, our engineering team works directly with the factory floors in Asia to ensure your product is built right.

Melbourne-Led Design

Product Design & Development

We bridge the gap between brilliant ideas and manufacturing reality. Our local engineering team refines your concepts into robust, production-ready assemblies.

What We Do

  • Industrial Design
  • Mechanical Eng. (CAD)
  • DFM / DFA Analysis
  • Certification

The Result

Our Melbourne team manages the risk, so you get a production-ready package without the usual overseas headaches.

Rapid Prototyping

Move fast and break things—but do it smartly. We iterate physical models quickly to validate mechanics, ergonomics, and aesthetics before investing in hard tooling.

What We Do

  • SLA / SLS 3D Printing
  • CNC Machining
  • Vacuum Casting
  • Gateway Management

The Result

Your local bridge to global manufacturing ensures functional, looks-like/works-like prototypes in your hands in days, not weeks.

Boots on the Ground

Strategic Sourcing & Supply Chain

Stop guessing on Alibaba. We leverage our network of pre-audited factories in Asia, handling RFQs and negotiations directly in the local language.

What We Do

  • Supplier Qualification
  • Vendor Comparison
  • Factory Audits
  • Logistics & Shipping

The Result

Melbourne expertise with Asian efficiency. We secure the best pricing while strictly enforcing IP protection and quality agreements.

Contract Manufacturing

When tooling is cut and mass production begins, you need eyes on the factory floor. We oversee production to guarantee the golden sample standard is met on every unit.

What We Do

  • Mass Production Overseeing
  • Quality Control (AQL)
  • Tooling Management
  • Pre-Shipment Inspection

The Result

We are your local safety net. We deal with the late-night calls and factory issues so you can focus on selling your product.

Electronics Development

Factory Automation
