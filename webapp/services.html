Our Services

Western product design rigour meets Asian manufacturing efficiency. From first sketch to scaled production, we deliver end-to-end solutions that bridge the gap between innovation and execution.

Product Design & Development

Western-trained industrial designers and mechanical engineers take your idea from napkin sketch to production-ready 3D CAD — with Design for Manufacturing baked in from day one.

What We Do

  • Concept Generation: Translating market insights and user needs into viable product architectures through structured ideation and competitive benchmarking.
  • Industrial Design: Creating compelling form factors that balance aesthetics, ergonomics, and manufacturing constraints.
  • Mechanical Engineering: Detailed 3D CAD (SolidWorks, NX) with integrated FEA, tolerance stack-ups, and DFM analysis before any tooling commitment.
  • Compliance Coordination: Turnkey management of CE, FCC, RoHS, UL, and market-specific regulatory certifications.

The Result

A production-ready design package with minimised tooling risk, certified for your target markets and optimised for Asian manufacturing processes.

Rapid Prototyping

SLA, SLS, CNC, and soft tooling — validate form, fit, and function in days, not months. Iterate fast with our in-house and partner facilities across Asia.

What We Do

  • Additive Manufacturing: SLA resin, SLS nylon, and FDM prototypes for functional testing and stakeholder review within 48–72 hours.
  • CNC Machining: Precision aluminium and steel parts for mechanical validation and pre-production fixtures.
  • Soft Tooling: Low-volume silicone molds and bridge tooling for market testing runs of 50–500 units.
  • Gateway Management: Structured EVT → DVT → PVT progression ensuring each prototype iteration closes critical unknowns.

The Result

Faster design convergence and de-risked tooling investment through validated prototypes that prove out every critical assumption before mass production.

Strategic Sourcing & Supply Chain

Leverage our vetted supplier network across the Pearl River Delta. We handle procurement, vendor qualification, logistics, and quality audits so you don't have to.

What We Do

  • Supplier Qualification: Rigorous factory auditing for capacity, IP protection, compliance standards, and financial stability.
  • Multi-Vendor Comparison: Competitive benchmarking across our network to guarantee authentic market-rate COGS.
  • Origin Diversification: Mitigating tariff and geopolitical risk by sourcing across China, Vietnam, India, and beyond.
  • Logistics Management: End-to-end freight coordination, customs clearance, and warehouse-to-door delivery.

The Result

A resilient, cost-optimised supply chain that protects your margins regardless of macroeconomic volatility.

Contract Manufacturing

Full-service production management — injection molding, die casting, CNC, finishing, assembly, and packaging. Western project methodology with Asian production efficiency and cost structure.

What We Do

  • Injection Molding & Tooling: Precision mold design, build, and qualification with T0–T2 sample management and PPAP documentation.
  • Multi-Process Assembly: Coordinating plastics, metals, electronics, and soft goods into a single finished-goods stream.
  • Quality Deployment: In-line IQC, IPQC, and OQC inspection with SKU-specific AQL plans and CAPA protocols.
  • Production Transfer: Structured NPI handover from prototype shop to volume factory, including operator training and process validation.

The Result

Defect-free production runs delivered on schedule, with Western-grade project controls applied to Asia's most competitive manufacturing base.

Electronics Development

PCB layout, firmware, PCBA assembly and testing. From IoT sensor modules to complex control systems — designed to international standards, manufactured at scale.

What We Do

  • Schematic & PCB Design: Multi-layer board design with signal integrity analysis, EMC pre-compliance, and thermal management.
  • Firmware Development: Embedded C/C++, BLE, Wi-Fi, and sensor integration from prototype to production-grade code.
  • PCBA Manufacturing: SMT pick-and-place, reflow soldering, AOI inspection, and functional test fixture development.
  • Certification Support: Pre-compliance testing and documentation for FCC, CE, IC, and MIC approvals.

The Result

Fully certified, production-ready electronics — from bare board to programmed, tested, and packaged PCBA modules ready for system integration.

Factory Automation

Custom jigs, fixtures, robotic cells, and production line automation. Reduce cycle times and labour costs while maintaining consistent, repeatable quality.

What We Do

  • Custom Fixtures & Jigs: Bespoke workholding, alignment, and test fixtures that eliminate operator variability.
  • Robotic Integration: SCARA, 6-axis, and collaborative robot cells for assembly, dispensing, and palletising operations.
  • PLC & Vision Systems: Programmable logic control, machine vision inspection, and conveyor system design for throughput optimisation.
  • Line Design: End-to-end production line architecture from material flow analysis to commissioning and operator training.

The Result

Higher throughput, lower defect rates, and reduced dependency on manual labour — making your Asian manufacturing partner even more competitive.
