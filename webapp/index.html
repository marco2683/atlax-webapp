Manufacturing Capabilities

Our Expertise.

From first prototype to mass production — every technology you need, under one roof of relationships.

Most Requested

Rapid Prototyping

From CAD to physical part in 48–72 hours. SLA, SLS, FDM, CNC soft tooling — iterate at full speed.

  • SLA & SLS resin — tolerances to ±0.1mm
  • FDM in ABS, PLA, Nylon, Carbon-fibre composites
  • CNC soft tooling for bridge production runs
  • Vacuum casting — up to 20 copies from one master
  • First Article Inspection (FAI) report included

Tooling & Injection Moulding

P20, H13 & soft aluminium tooling. DFM embedded from sketch to steel.

  • P20 (100k+ shots), H13 (500k+ shots), 1.2316
  • Mould-flow simulation before any steel is cut
  • Multi-cavity, family, and hot-runner tooling
  • ABS, PC, PA66, TPU, POM, PEEK

CNC Machining

3, 4 & 5-axis precision for metals and plastics. Tolerances to ±0.01mm.

  • Al 6061/7075, SS 316L, Ti6Al4V, PEEK, Delrin
  • Anodising, bead-blast, hard chrome, black oxide
  • Turning, milling, EDM wire & sinker

Metal Fabrication

Enclosures, frames, brackets — cut, formed, finished at any volume.

  • Laser cutting up to 25mm mild steel, 12mm SS
  • CNC press brake, JIS / DIN compliance
  • Progressive die stamping from 10k pcs

Electronics & PCBA

Schematic to tested board. IPC Class 2 & 3 with in-line AOI and burn-in.

  • 4-layer to 16-layer HDI PCB design & layout
  • SMT 0201 / BGA / QFN & through-hole assembly
  • AOI + X-ray BGA verification
  • UL/CE sourcing; BOM cost optimisation

Factory Automation

Custom robotics, PLCs & assembly lines.

  • PLC & Robotics integration
  • Custom assembly lines
  • End-of-arm tooling
  • Automated quality inspection

Soft Goods

Technical fabrics & wearable integration.

  • Cut and sew manufacturing
  • Technical fabrics & textiles
  • Sonic welding & thermoforming
  • Wearables integration

Project Quote & Instant Parts Quote

Two quoting modes for every stage. Instant Quote gives you real, pre-negotiated prices for standard manufactured components — upload your CAD and get a number in minutes. Project Quote lets our team go deep on complex assemblies, BOMs, and multi-part projects with human-backed analysis.
