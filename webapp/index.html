Technical Capabilities

Deep engineering across every discipline.

Our team has hands-on experience across the full spectrum of hardware manufacturing:
  • Tooling & Injection Moulding: Custom mould design, multi-cavity tools, hot/cold runner systems, and high-volume plastic part production. We oversee every T0–T2 trial on the factory floor.
  • CNC Machining & Metal Fabrication: Multi-axis CNC milling, turning, EDM, and precision sheet metal in aluminium, steel, titanium, and exotic alloys.
  • Electronics & PCBA Development: Schematic capture, PCB layout, firmware, embedded systems, SMT assembly, and compliance testing (CE, FCC, UL).
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM): We analyse every part for mouldability, wall thickness, draft angles, and tolerance stacking to eliminate costly revisions.
  • Surface Finishing & Post-Processing: Anodising, powder coating, electroplating, painting, pad printing, and laser engraving — managed and QC’d on-site.
  • Assembly & Box-Build: Turnkey assembly lines, custom jigs & fixtures, functional testing rigs, and retail-ready packaging.
  • Quality Assurance & Compliance: ISO 9001 / 13485 processes, incoming material inspection, in-line QC, CMM measurement, and pre-shipment audits.
